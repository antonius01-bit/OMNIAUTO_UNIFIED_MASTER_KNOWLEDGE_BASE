## 🛠️ Reusable Analytical Recipes

### 1. Earned Value Management (EVM) Calculator
\text{CV} = \text{EV} - \text{AC}, \quad \text{SV} = \text{EV} - \text{PV}
\text{CPI} = \frac{\text{EV}}{\text{AC}}, \quad \text{SPI} = \frac{\text{EV}}{\text{PV}}
\text{EAC} = \frac{\text{BAC}}{\text{CPI}}, \quad \text{VAC} = \text{BAC} - \text{EAC}

### 2. RACI Responsibility Assignment Matrix
- **R - Responsible**: Pelaksana tugas utama.
- **A - Accountable**: Pemegang keputusan akhir dan otorisasi.
- **C - Consulted**: Pakar rujukan dua arah sebelum eksekusi.
- **I - Informed**: Pihak terkait yang diperbarui secara satu arah.

### 3. MoSCoW Feature Categorization
- **Must Have**: Persyaratan vital tanpa kompromi (MVP).
- **Should Have**: Fitur penting namun ada solusi sementara.
- **Could Have**: Fitur bernilai tambah jika waktu/anggaran tersisa.
- **Won't Have (This Time)**: Ditunda ke sprint berikutnya.
