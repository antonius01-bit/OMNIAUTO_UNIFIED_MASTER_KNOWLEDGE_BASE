# S74: Psychometric Assessment Suite (psychometric-assessment-suite)

## ⚡ Overview & Clinical/Organizational Battery
Synthesized from C:\Users\antoni\Downloads\s2 mm\hr\29 Aplikasi Tools Psikotes Terbaru:
1. **Cognitive & Intelligence Batteries**:
   - **CFIT (Culture Fair Intelligence Test)**: Scale 2A/2B & 3A/3B fluid intelligence measurement.
   - **IST (Intelligenz Struktur Test)**: 9 subtests (SE, WA, AN, GE, ME, RA, ZR, FA, WU) measuring crystallized vs fluid intelligence.
   - **SPM (Standard Progressive Matrices)** & **APM**: Abstract non-verbal reasoning.
   - **TIU 5-6 & Army Alpha**: General intelligence and verbal-logical reasoning.
   - **WPT (Wonderlic Personnel Test)**: Rapid cognitive problem-solving.
2. **Personality & Leadership Inventories**:
   - **DISC**: Dominance, Influence, Steadiness, Conscientiousness behavioral profiling.
   - **MBTI**: 16 Myers-Briggs personality preferences.
   - **Papikostik (Kostick Life Style Inventory)**: 20 personality dimensions across work direction, leadership, activity, social nature, work style, temperament, and position follow.
   - **16 PF (Cattell 16 Personality Factors)**: Deep structural trait assessment.
   - **BFI (Big Five Inventory)**: OCEAN taxonomy (Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, Neuroticism).
   - **MSDT (Management Style Diagnostic Test)**: 3-D leadership theory (Task vs Relationship orientation).
3. **Endurance & Speed Tests**:
   - **Kraeplin & Pauli Test**: Work speed (*Arbeitsgeschwindigkeit*), accuracy, stability, endurance, and stress resistance under pressure.
4. **Projective & Clinical Diagnostics**:
   - **Wartegg Drawing Test (WZT)**: 8-stimulus drawing interpretation (Ego, emotion, ambition, anxiety, social adaptability).
   - **MMPI (Minnesota Multiphasic Personality Inventory)**: Clinical psychological assessment and screening.
   - **BDI (Beck Depression Inventory)**: Affective mood evaluation.
   - **RMIB (Rothwell Miller Interest Blank)** & **Holland (RIASEC)** & **Kuder**: Vocational interest inventories.

## 🛠️ Organizational Application
- Assessment Center battery design matched to job levels (Entry, Specialist, Managerial, Executive).
- Norm-referenced scoring and automated profiling reports.
