# S88: NVivo Qualitative & AI Research Engine (
vivo-qualitative-ai-research)

## ⚡ Overview & Research Taxonomy
Synthesized from Beginner: Penelitian Kualitatif Menggunakan NVIVO, Educativa AI Webinar, and Prompting Training modules:
1. **Qualitative Data Coding in NVivo**:
   - Node hierarchy: Parent nodes (themes) and child nodes (sub-themes/codes).
   - In-vivo coding, descriptive coding, and axial coding.
   - Text search queries, word frequency clouds, and matrix coding queries.
2. **AI-Assisted Thematic Analysis**:
   - Grounded theory coding supported by LLM thematic clustering.
   - Cross-case comparisons, demographic attribute classification, and sentiment polarity scoring.
3. **Mixed-Methods Triangulation (Kuantitatif vs Kualitatif)**:
   - Convergent parallel, explanatory sequential, and exploratory sequential designs.
   - Data quality optimization through structured prompt engineering.
