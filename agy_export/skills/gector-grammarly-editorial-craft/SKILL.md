# S112: GECToR Grammarly Editorial Craft Engine (gector-grammarly-editorial-craft)

## ⚡ Overview & Editorial Science
Synthesized from Grammarly Research (`grammarly/gector` - "Tag, Not Rewrite" SOTA architecture) and open-source writing engines (`riponcm/GemType`, `itallstartedwithaidea/writing-agent`):
1. **GECToR Sequence Tagging Methodology**:
   - Rather than unpredictable generative rewriting that hallucinates arguments, GECToR uses sequence tagging ($tag-based edit operations: $KEEP, $DELETE, $APPEND, $REPLACE, $TRANSFORM).
   - Preserves 100% of original authorial voice, academic citations `[N#E]`, equations, and domain terminology while eliminating grammatical, prepositional, and subject-verb agreement errors.
2. **40-Point Academic Editorial Quality Assurance**:
   - Sentence complexity rhythm (avoiding monotonous cadence).
   - Passive vs active voice calibration (formal academic voice without bureaucratic bloat).
   - Transition coherence and paragraph topic sentence alignment.
3. **Multilingual Fluency & GemType Anti-Error Guard**:
   - Eliminates translation artifacts between Indonesian academic style and Scopus-indexed English standards.
   - Punctuation precision: Oxford commas, en-dash vs em-dash, semicolon logic, and quotation placement.

## 🛠️ Usage in Omni-Auto
- **Command**: `/omni-auto gector edit: Polish academic manuscript grammar, fluency, and syntax tags for [draft_text]`
- Enforced during Phase 3 & 4 (Synthesis, Publication Shield, and Academic Polishing).
