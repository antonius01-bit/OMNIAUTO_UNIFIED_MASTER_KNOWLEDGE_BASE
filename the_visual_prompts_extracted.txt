CREATIVE REFERENCE  /  2026 EDITION  /  13 COLLECTIONS  /  ONE PRACTICAL FIELD GUIDE
A short label. A clearer starting
point.
Treat each shortcut as the name of a
creative brief. Give it context, then iterate.
HOW TO USE THE BOOK
01 Define the shortcut. Paste its
description into your chat, or attach this
guide and ask the chat to use its
definitions.
02 Add the ingredients. Supply the
subject, reference image, exact text, visual
style and output shape. Say what must stay
unchanged.
03 Review and refine. Check faces,
product details, words and layout. Ask for
one focused change, then compare the
result.
YOUR FIRST MESSAGE
"Use /headshot to mean a polished
professional portrait. Use my photo; keep
my features. Soft studio light, simple
background, square crop."
A slash label is shorthand, not a promise of
a built-in command. A connected service
may offer a matching preset; ask it to
resolve the name first.
Use PDF bookmarks to jump straight to a
collection.
