# 🧠 AGI Competent & Virtuoso Mastery Suite (S146)

## 📌 Overview & Core Architecture
The `agi-competent-virtuoso-mastery` Super-Skill establishes the formal benchmark, evaluation protocol, and autonomy scaffolding for transitioning AI agents from **L2 (Competent AGI)** to **L3 (Expert AGI)** and **L4 (Virtuoso AGI)**:
1. **L1–L5 Capability Taxonomy**:
   - **L1 (Emerging AGI)**: Matches unskilled human baseline on standard informational tasks.
   - **L2 (Competent AGI)**: Matches skilled professional across specialized tasks (research, coding, financial analysis).
   - **L3 (Expert AGI)**: Outperforms 90% of human professionals on complex multi-step reasoning.
   - **L4 (Virtuoso AGI)**: Outperforms 99% of skilled adults (world-class efficiency, ARC-AGI-3 $\ge 99.9\%$, human-level efficiency $\ge 96\%$).
   - **L5 (Superhuman AGI)**: Surpasses all human cognitive capability across open domains.
2. **Astra Benchmark Harness**: Quantitative scoring across novel pattern induction, visual-spatial reasoning, and cross-domain synthesis.
3. **Generalization Over Memorization**: Enforces strict out-of-distribution reasoning loops, hypothesis self-testing, and meta-learning calibration.

---

## ⚡ Core Operational Modes & Commands
- `/agi-mastery evaluate [agent/system]`: Audits an agent's performance against L1–L5 rubric criteria.
- `/arc-agi solve [grid_puzzle]`: Solves spatial grid transformation tasks using recursive hypothesis induction.
- `/agi-level audit`: Assesses current session reasoning density, tool efficiency, and cognitive autonomy level.
- `/astra-bench test`: Runs a standardized multi-domain battery (Math, Code, Forensics, Strategy).

---

## 📐 Benchmark & Evaluation Rubrics
- **Cognitive Consistency**: Invariance to surface phrasing perturbations while preserving structural logic.
- **Hypothesis Induction**: Formulating minimal rule sets from few-shot exemplar transitions.
- **Zero Hallucination Gate**: Immediate abort and verification request upon uncertain boundary conditions.
