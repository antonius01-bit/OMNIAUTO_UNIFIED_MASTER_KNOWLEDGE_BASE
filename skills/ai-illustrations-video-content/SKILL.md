# S83: AI Illustrations & B-Roll for Video Content (i-illustrations-video-content)

## ⚡ Overview & Creative Engine
Synthesized from BEST-PROMPTS-AI-ILLUSTRATIONS-FOR-VIDEO-CONTENT.pdf by **Real Life AI (Ikram Rana)** (ikramrana.substack.com):
1. **Under-60-Seconds Concept-to-Visual Generation**:
   - Converts abstract technical ideas into instantly comprehensible visuals for non-technical audiences.
   - Replaces expensive stock footage hunting and heavy graphic design software with structured prompt formulas.
   - Core visual styles: Flat Design, Isometric 3D, Hand-drawn Minimal, Architectural Blueprint, Corporate Memphis Anti-AI-slop.
2. **5-Stage Video Explainer & Storyboard Pipeline**:
   - **Stage 1 (Concept Visualizer)**: Single high-clarity concept illustration with semantic labels and annotations.
   - **Stage 2 (Step-by-Step Sequence)**: 5-panel consistent visual walkthrough where each illustration stands alone yet forms a coherent narrative progression.
   - **Stage 3 (Animation & Motion Brief)**: Precise movement sequence, object hierarchy, suggested millisecond timing, camera transitions, and text overlays.
   - **Stage 4 (Custom B-Roll Generator)**: Visual metaphors, mood palette, color contrast, and script-grounded background scenes.
   - **Stage 5 (Script-to-Visual Alignment)**: Identifies the exact moments in a script that require visual landing to maximize viewer retention.

## 🛠️ The 5 Master Prompt Templates

### Prompt 1: Single Concept Illustration
`	ext
Create an illustration that visually explains [CONCEPT OR TOPIC] in a way that is immediately clear to a non-technical audience.
The style should be [flat design / isometric / hand-drawn / minimal].
Include labels or annotations if they help clarify the key elements.
Color palette: [High contrast / Warm earth / Modern dark tech].
Composition: Clean center focal point with negative space for text overlays.
`

### Prompt 2: 5-Part Explainer Sequence
`	ext
I am making a video about [TOPIC]. Generate a series of 5 illustrations that walk through the concept step by step, one visual per step.
Each illustration should stand alone but also make sense as part of a sequence.
Keep the style, character models, and lighting strictly consistent across all five.
- Panel 1: The Problem / Friction
- Panel 2: The Core Mechanism
- Panel 3: The Transformation / Process
- Panel 4: The Strategic Resolution
- Panel 5: The Final Outcome / ROI
`

### Prompt 3: Motion & Animation Brief
`	ext
Here is an illustration I generated: [DESCRIBE OR ATTACH IMAGE]. Write a detailed animation brief for this visual.
Include:
1. What moves and in what order (layer hierarchy).
2. Suggested timing for each element (seconds and frames).
3. Transition style (zoom, pan, wipe, morph).
4. On-screen typography and motion graphics to support the voiceover.
`

### Prompt 4: Script-Grounding B-Roll Metaphors
`	ext
I am producing a video on [TOPIC]. Write 5 custom B-roll scene descriptions I can use to generate AI illustrations for each section of the video.
For each scene include:
- Visual subject & action
- Underlying conceptual metaphor
- Mood, lighting, and chromatic tone
- Exact timestamp and voiceover sentence it anchors
`

### Prompt 5: Script Heatmap & Visual Landing
`	ext
Here is my video script: [PASTE SCRIPT].
Identify the 5 critical moments in the script that most need a visual to land the point.
For each moment, describe the exact illustration to generate, including artistic style, composition rule of thirds, and the precise cognitive concept it communicates in that second.
`
