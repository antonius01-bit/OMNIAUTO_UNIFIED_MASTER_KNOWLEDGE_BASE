# 🔬 Open Researcher & Scientific Autonomy Suite (S139)

## 📌 Overview & Core Architecture
The `open-researcher-scientific-autonomy` Super-Skill empowers AI agents to act as fully autonomous research scholars, combining the state-of-the-art open research frameworks:
1. **alphaXiv OpenResearch**: Community-driven, open-access academic discourse, inline arXiv paper annotation, and preprint verification.
2. **TIGER-AI-Lab OpenResearcher**: Agentic scientific research workflow orchestrator capable of iterative hypothesis formulation, paper retrieval, methodological breakdown, and experimental synthesis.
3. **OpenAgent Ecosystem (`the-open-agent/openagent` & `openagents-org/openagents`)**: Modular multi-agent research crews equipped with citation graph crawlers, LaTeX drafting engines, and peer-review simulators.

---

## ⚡ Core Operational Modes & Commands
- `/open-research search [query]`: Execute high-density multi-database academic paper search across arXiv, OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, and Europe PMC.
- `/open-researcher formulate-hypothesis [topic]`: Generate falsifiable scientific hypotheses mapped to Toulmin argument structures and Bradford Hill causality criteria.
- `/arxiv-autonomy annotate [arxiv_id]`: Fetch full preprint LaTeX/HTML, extract math proofs, and generate line-by-line critical evaluations.
- `/scientific-synthesis review [papers...]`: Synthesize a comprehensive PRISMA-compliant literature review table with verified DOIs and synchronized RIS export.

---

## 🔬 Autonomous Scientific Loop
1. **Phase 1: Exploratory Query Formulation**:
   - Deconstruct research problem into ontological entities, causal pathways, and boundary conditions.
   - Expand query terms using MeSH keywords, ACM Computing Classification, and arXiv category taxonomies.
2. **Phase 2: Deep Citation Graph Traversal**:
   - Query forward citations (papers citing the work) and backward citations (papers referenced).
   - Identify foundational papers, seminal shifts, and emerging state-of-the-art breakthroughs.
3. **Phase 3: Critical Methodological Audit**:
   - Verify sample sizes, statistical power ($p$-values, confidence intervals, effect sizes).
   - Flag potential methodological biases, data leakage, and unverified benchmark claims.
4. **Phase 4: Synthesis & Output Generation**:
   - Structured LaTeX/Markdown manuscript drafts with exact `[N#E]` citation markers.
   - Direct synchronization with Mendeley/Zotero `.ris` metadata databases.
