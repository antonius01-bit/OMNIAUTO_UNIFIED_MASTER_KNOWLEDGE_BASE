# 🎓 Thesis Super-Pipeline Orchestrator (S153)

## 📌 Overview & Core Architecture
The `thesis-super-pipeline-orchestrator` Super-Skill coordinates the complete 8-stage academic research and publication engine defined in `ULTIMATE-FREE-STACK + AGI-MASTERY v3.0.md`:
1. **Search**: Multi-database literature search (arXiv, Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex, Europe PMC).
2. **Analyze**: Empirical data analysis via Jamovi, PSPP, or GNU Octave with verified statistical variables.
3. **Write**: Dual-language manuscript drafting (Indonesian & English) with synchronized `[N#E]` claims.
4. **Proof**: Academic fluency, syntax tagging, and anti-AI humanization (GECToR, FK-17).
5. **Cite**: Exact citation grounding with verified authentic DOIs and author metadata (FK-19).
6. **RIS**: Automated generation of `Daftar Pustaka — Mendeley.ris` with matching reference IDs.
7. **Mendeley**: 1-click import synchronization into Mendeley, Zotero, and EndNote.
8. **Export**: Generation of 4 guaranteed functional deliverables (`.docx` ID, `.docx` EN, `.pptx`, `.ris`).

---

## ⚡ Core Operational Modes & Commands
- `/thesis-pipeline execute [topic]`: Runs the complete 8-step autonomous pipeline from research question to final deliverables.
- `/thesis-run chapter [1-5]`: Drafts or refines an individual thesis chapter following university guidelines.
- `/academic-flow cite-verify`: Audits all citations in a manuscript against Semantic Scholar and CrossRef APIs.
- `/thesis-sync mendeley`: Exports and validates the synchronized `.ris` file.

## 🔄 The 8-Stage Research Lifecycle
$$\text{Search} \longrightarrow \text{Analyze} \longrightarrow \text{Write} \longrightarrow \text{Proof} \longrightarrow \text{Cite} \longrightarrow \text{RIS} \longrightarrow \text{Mendeley} \longrightarrow \text{Export}$$
- Every factual claim is bound to a verified source: $[N\#E] \longleftrightarrow \text{ID - } N$.
- Triple-protection shield active at all stages: FK-16 (Similarity $\le 5\%$), FK-17 (Anti-AI Clichés), FK-19 (Authentic DOIs).
