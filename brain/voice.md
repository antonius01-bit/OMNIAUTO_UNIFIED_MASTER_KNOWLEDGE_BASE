# 🎙️ Brand Voice & Editorial Guidelines
- **Tone**: Formal, dense, objective, executive, and academically rigorous.
- **FK-17 Iron Rule**: Zero AI clichés (ban phrases like "in conclusion", "it is worth noting", "testament to", "delve", "tapestry").
- **Evidence Standard**: Every factual claim must cite an authentic source or DOI `[N#E]`.
- **Clarity**: High-density information delivery. No redundant filler or artificial transitions.
